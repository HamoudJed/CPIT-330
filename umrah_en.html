Ihram

The first step in Umrah: entering the state of consecration by wearing Ihram garments at Miqat

Essential Steps:

  • Wear Ihram garments (Izar and Rida for men)
  • Make intention: "Labbayk Allahumma Umrah"
  • Recite Talbiyah: "Labbayk Allahumma labbayk, labbayk la shareeka laka labbayk, innal-hamda wan-ni'mata laka wal-mulk, la shareeka lak"

Sunnah & Recommended Acts:

  • Take a ritual bath (Ghusl) before Ihram
  • Apply perfume to body before Ihram (not on clothes)
  • It is recommended to enter Ihram after an obligatory prayer if possible, or after ablution prayer
  • Recite Talbiyah frequently, especially when changing situations
2

Tawaf

Circumambulation around the Kaaba seven times, starting and ending at the Black Stone

Essential Steps:

  • Ritual purity (Wudu and Ghusl) is required for valid Tawaf
  • Touch or point to the Black Stone saying "Allahu Akbar"
  • Complete 7 rounds counter-clockwise
  • Make supplications during Tawaf
  • Say "Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanah wa fil-akhirati hasanah" between the two corners
  • Pray 2 Rak'ahs after Tawaf (behind Maqam if possible)

Sunnah & Recommended Acts:

  • Kiss the Black Stone if possible without causing harm
  • Idtiba (uncovering right shoulder) and Raml (brisk walking with short steps) for men in first 3 rounds
  • Touch the Yemeni Corner if possible
  • Make abundant supplications and remembrance of Allah

Sa'i between Safa and Marwah

Walking seven times between the hills of Safa and Marwah

Essential Steps:

  • Start from Safa
  • Recite: "Inna as-Safa wal-Marwata min sha'a'irillah"
  • Walk to Marwah (this is one round)
  • Return from Marwah to Safa (second round)
  • Complete 7 rounds (ending at Marwah)
  • Make supplications during Sa'i

Sunnah & Recommended Acts:

  • Ascend upon Safa and Marwah and face the Qiblah
  • Jog briskly between the green markers (men only)
  • Increase remembrance of Allah and supplications, especially upon Safa and Marwah

Note: Ritual purity (Wudu) is recommended but not required for Sa'i. Sa'i performed without ablution is still valid.

Halq or Taqsir

Shaving the head completely or trimming the hair, with shaving being better for men

Essential Steps:

  • Men: Shave head (preferred) or trim hair from all sides
  • Women: Trim a fingertip's length from hair ends
  • With this, Umrah is complete and all Ihram restrictions are lifted

Important Notes:

  • Shaving is better than trimming for men, except if Hajj is approaching soon - then trimming is better to leave hair for Hajj
  • Must cover all areas of the head
  • There is no specific authentic supplication after shaving; one may say "Allahumma taqabbal minna" (O Allah, accept from us)
